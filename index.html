Your trolley

Why three shops? Bunnings doesn't sell 50 x 50 x 3 mm aluminium tube, 100 mm aluminium flat bar or a 20 mm round bar long enough for the axle. The metal comes from RHS Steel Supplies in Sydney, which cuts it to length (any aluminium supplier sells these sizes). Bunnings only sells stainless M8 x 75 bolts in 2-packs, so the bolts and the axle washers are one small online order from Stainless Central. Everything else comes from Bunnings.
