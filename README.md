# Boat Dolly

A LEGO-style build booklet and shopping trolley for an aluminium A-frame launching dolly, sized for a Sabot (or another small boat up to 80 kg).

**Open it:** https://cart.seankoji.com/

**Second booklet:** a 30 cm wallaby built from real LEGO bricks, with 32 steps and a BrickLink parts list: https://cart.seankoji.com/wallaby/ ([source](wallaby/)).

It's a single self-contained `index.html`: 44 illustrated steps with isometric drawings, 1:1 printable drilling templates, a hull-cradle you shape to your own boat, a loading guide, a load test, and a shopping list split by store (RHS Steel Supplies, Stainless Central, Bunnings), with a link to each product.

## Budget

The parts come to about **$634 AUD** at listed prices on 8 November 2026, plus delivery for one small online bolt order, inside a $650 budget. Bunnings doesn't sell the aluminium tube, flat bar or axle rod, so the metal comes from a metal supplier that cuts it to length. Bunnings only sells stainless M8 x 75 bolts in 2-packs, so the bolts and axle washers come from an online fastener shop. Everything else is from Bunnings. The trolley has a budget meter, and you can type in real prices as you get quotes. Its money-saving ideas can take about $150 off. Tools and drill bits aren't included.

## Before you build

- **Not load-tested.** The design came from a parts list and sketches. Run the load-test page before it carries a boat.
- **Prices change.** They were checked against the linked listings on 8 November 2026. Check prices and stock with each store.
- **A grown-up does every saw, drill, knife, glue and flame step.**

## Print the templates

Open the Templates page, print at 100% (turn off "fit to page"), and check the 50 mm line on each template measures 50 mm.
